Pre-orders

  • What we keep: the email address you type in, which option you picked (free trial, monthly or forever) and the time. Not your name, not your IP address, not which page you came from. Nothing is charged.
  • Why: to send you one email when what you picked is ready. Nothing else is sent to it, and it is never sold or shared.
  • Where: in storage on our own Cloudflare account (Cloudflare Workers KV), which only we can read. Cloudflare processes it as our host.
  • How long: until that email is sent, then deleted, or sooner if you ask.
  • To be removed: write to [SUPPORT EMAIL] from the same address and we will delete it within [N] days.

What we hold if you buy

  • Your email address, because we have to send you a key.
  • The licence key we issued to it, so we can re-send it if you lose it.
  • Your payment record, which is held by [PROCESSOR], not by us. We never see or store card details.

We do not hold anything you say to Friday, anything she answers, your files, your contacts, or any record that she is running.

What stays on your computer

All of it. Her memory, your reminders, timers, notes and lists, your contacts, the name you chose for her, and her record of what she has sent. These are ordinary files in her data folder; you can read them, back them up or delete them, and nothing syncs anywhere.

Each person on a machine has their own account, and what Friday learns is kept under that account and tied to that install — one account never reads another's memory, and a data folder copied to a different device is not adopted as anyone's. You can erase everything she has learned about you in one step: tell her "delete my account" and, once you confirm, she wipes that account's memory, saved secrets and all — permanently, on the spot.

What leaves your computer, and when

This is the part most assistants are vague about, so here it is plainly.

  • Easy questions never leave. Reminders, timers, alarms, notes, lists, opening apps, volume, brightness, windows, files, the clock — answered on your machine, with the network untouched.
  • Hard questions do leave. They go to whichever AI you are signed into — Claude, Codex or Gemini — through the CLI you already pay for. Your question travels with them, and their privacy policy applies to it, not ours.
  • Before anything leaves, identifiers are stripped. Names in your contacts, phone numbers, emails and card numbers are replaced with placeholders and restored on your machine afterwards. The mapping never leaves the process.
  • Some things never leave at all. Aadhaar, PAN, passport, card, IFSC and GSTIN numbers are withheld even in masked form, because masking one still reveals that it exists. She answers from her own reasoning instead, and says that she did.
  • Web searches leave. If you ask her to look something up she contacts the search engine or site directly, as your browser would.
  • Smart-home commands go to your own Home Assistant on your own network.

Every answer tells you which of these happened. That indicator is the product.

The honest limit

The stripping catches patterns and the names you have written down, not meaning. "The patient with the limp who runs the bakery on Fourth Street" goes out intact. So the truthful claim is that identifiers are stripped — never that the model cannot work out who someone is. If something must never be seen at all, do not send it: sealed mode keeps anything personal on your machine.

No tracking, and what that means

This website runs no tracking scripts and sets no cookies; the only thing it ever sends us is what you choose to type into the pre-order form.

Inside Friday there is no analytics service, no telemetry, no crash reporting and no licence check over the network. Her licence key is verified by a signature on your own machine. We genuinely cannot tell whether you have installed her, opened her, or stopped using her.
