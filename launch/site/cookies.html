Cookies

Last updated 13 November 2026 · checked by loading every page and reading what it stored

The short version: this website sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. That is why there is no cookie banner: there is nothing to accept or refuse.

What this site stores on your device

KindUsed here?
CookiesNo
Local storage, session storage, IndexedDBNo
Tracking pixels, analytics, advertising scriptsNo
Fonts, scripts or images from other companiesNo. Everything is served from this site, so loading it contacts no one else

The page remembers nothing between visits. If you scroll halfway and come back tomorrow, it starts from the top.

The preview form

When you ask for an invite, what you typed is sent once to our storage and nothing is saved in your browser. What we keep, and how to have it deleted, is on the privacy page.

What our host could add

The site is served by Cloudflare. Cloudflare can add its own security cookies, such as __cf_bm or cf_clearance, but only when a site owner turns on bot protection or a challenge page. We have not turned any of these on. If we ever do, the cookie will be listed here, with its purpose and how long it lasts, before it is switched on.

Friday herself

Friday is a Windows program, not a website, so cookies do not apply to her. What she keeps is in ordinary files in her data folder on your computer; the privacy page describes them.
